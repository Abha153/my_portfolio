Before We Start

How long will a portfolio take?

The timeline depends on the number of sections, content readiness, and requested features. I’ll agree on a delivery date before starting.

What do I need to provide?

Please share your text, project links, resume or profile details, preferred style, and any reference websites you like.

What counts as a revision?

A revision is a reasonable adjustment to the agreed design or content. New pages, major redesigns, and features outside the agreed scope may cost extra.

Are domain and hosting included?

Deployment to a suitable free hosting option is included where specified. A custom domain, paid hosting, and third-party service fees are separate.

How does payment work?

For agreed website projects, 50% is paid in advance and the remaining 50% before final handover. The scope and payment terms are confirmed before work begins.
